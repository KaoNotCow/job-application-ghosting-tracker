How verdicts and scores work

Speed score: 100 for a reply within a day, minus 5 points for every extra day, down to 0 at three weeks. Interviews get +15. Ghosted applications score 0. A company's score averages its replies. Frontline Education is held at "Left on Read" for its unanswered applications because it did interview you.
